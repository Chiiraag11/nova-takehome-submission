# Design

Replace each prompt line (the ones starting with `>`) with your own words. Keep the whole file to about 800 words.

## Fact model

> What do you store from each document, how is it keyed, and how do you know where each fact came from?

## Conflicts

> How do you notice that two sources disagree, and how do you decide whether it's a conflict or one source simply wins?

## Confidence

> How do you turn evidence, authority, extraction and agreement into one number, and how did you check it?

## People in the loop

> What goes to a person, what is drafted, and what may `autonomous` mode send on its own?

## Feedback

> How does a person's decision change later reports, and what stops it from spreading too far?

## New documents

> What happens when a batch brings an amendment, a new layout or a document that changes an earlier answer?

## Cost

> Your estimate of model cost and time per 1,000 documents, and how you got it.

## What I'd do next

> What's unfinished, what you'd change with another week, and what you're least sure about.
